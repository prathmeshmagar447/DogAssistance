About DoggyBot

We're a team of passionate dog lovers, veterinarians, and AI experts dedicated to helping you be the best dog parent you can be.

Our Mission

Our mission is to make expert dog care advice accessible to everyone, anytime. We believe that a well-informed owner leads to a happier, healthier dog. DoggyBot was born from a simple idea: what if you could ask a knowledgeable friend about your dog's behavior, health, or training at any hour of the day? We've combined cutting-edge AI technology with a vast database of veterinary-approved information to create a reliable and friendly assistant for all your dog-related questions.
