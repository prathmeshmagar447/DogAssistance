My puppy keeps chewing on my shoes. What can I do?

That's a common puppy behavior! Chewing is natural. To redirect this, provide plenty of appropriate chew toys. When you catch your puppy chewing shoes, say "No" firmly but calmly, and immediately offer a chew toy. Praise them when they take the toy.
